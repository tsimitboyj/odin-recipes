Fried rice

Description

Nigerian-style fried rice is a savory, flavorful dish made by cooking rice in seasoned meat stock and stir-frying it with a colorful mix of vegetables and diced protein

To begin, wash and parboil your raw rice, then cook it partially using rich chicken or turkey stock, curry powder, and thyme until the liquid is absorbed. In a large pan or wok, heat vegetable oil or butter to sauté diced onions, carrots, green beans, peas, and sweet corn, keeping the vegetables crisp and bright. Finally, fold in the pre-cooked rice and optional pre-fried liver, shrimp, or chicken pieces, stirring continuously on medium-high heat for a few minutes until all the flavors are thoroughly combined

Ingredients

  • parboiled long-grain rice
  • seasoned chicken or turkey stock
  • mixed vegetables
  • rich aromatics

Steps

  1. cook seasoned meat
  2. toast and steam the raw rice in rich broth
  3. stir-fry fresh vegetables
  4. combine everything together
