Jollof rice

Description

Jollof rice is a famous West African one-pot dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, and savory spices. It traces its roots back to the Wolof Empire in Senegal. Cooks fry a rich base of blended tomatoes, onions, and chili peppers in oil, then simmer the rice in seasoned meat or chicken stock.

This vibrant dish is the star of weddings, birthdays, and community celebrations across the region. Different countries add their own unique touch, leading to a friendly rivalry known as the Jollof wars. People often enjoy it hot with fried plantains and chicken.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Tomato/Pepper Blend
  • 2 large red onions (chopped/blended)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 thumb-sized ginger
  • 120g to 150g canned tomato paste
  • 2 to 3 cups rich beef or chicken stock, plus water
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil and 2 tablespoons butter
  • Spices & Seasonings

Steps

  1. Blend the Pepper Mix
  2. Parboil the Rice
  3. Fry the Base
  4. Add Pepper Puree
  5. Season
  6. Add Stock
  7. Cover and Seal
  8. Cook
